# GVS tier lists: research notes (compiled 2026-11-17)

GVS is Geoffrey Verity Schofield, YouTube channel https://www.youtube.com/@GVS. The structured data is in `gvs_tierlist.json`.

## The creator tier list: "The Science Based Fitness Tier List (Who's Legit?)"

- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HSPidQAsVmo
- Published: 2025-03-14
- Scale: S > A > B > C > D

**His 10 criteria (shown on screen):**
1. Research and its quality
2. "DYEL, bro?" (does the person lift)
3. Nuance vs dogmatism
4. How they react to criticism
5. Science used as marketing or rage bait
6. Educational background
7. Integrity
8. Selling out
9. Practical experience
10. Ratio of great to good to bad takes

**Placements (verified from the video's own frames):**

| Tier | Creators |

| S | Jeff Nippard, Eric Helms, Eric Bugenhagen, Data Driven Strength, Kassem Hansen, Lyle McDonald, Joe "Hypertrophy Coach" Bennett, House of Hypertrophy, Greg Nuckols, Jonathan Warren |

He also said verbally, in an on-screen caption, that Paul Carter is D tier.

Caveats:

- We watched 19 placements happen on the board during their segments. The last 6 (Israetel, Nuckols, Barakat, Warren, TNF, Galpin) were matched by photo to the final board.
